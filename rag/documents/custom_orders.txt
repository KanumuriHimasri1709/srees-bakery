Ordering & Customization Policy:
Lead Time: Cake orders must be placed 1 day before.
Dietary Preferences: Both Egg and Eggless options are available. However, eggless availability for a specific flavour must be confirmed with the bakery.
Customization Capabilities:
- Customized cakes
- Customized chocolates
- Theme cakes
- Pastries
- Brownie cake customization
Customers can submit their design ideas, themes, and optional reference images through the website enquiry form or by calling 7981468535 / 8801121818.
