Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q: Do you have eggless cakes?
A: Egg and eggless cake options are available. Eggless availability for a specific flavour should be confirmed with the bakery.

Q: Can I customize a cake?
A: Customized cakes, theme cakes, and customized chocolates are available. Cake orders should be placed 1 day before.

Q: Do you deliver and who pays for delivery?
A: Home delivery is available through Rapido. Delivery charges are paid by the customer.

Q: How can I place an order or enquiry?
A: Use the enquiry form on the website or call 7981468535 / 8801121818.

Q: What payment options do you support?
A: Payment is available through PhonePe.

Q: Do you have sugar-free or maida-free cookies?
A: Only Millet Cookies are explicitly confirmed as having no sugar and no maida. Do not generalize that claim to other products.
